Our Story

Kofièra began with a simple dream: a cozy corner where coffee is honest, affordable, and made with care. We're brand new — and we're just getting started.

From the first cup we pour to the first friends we meet, our mission is clear: make great coffee something you can enjoy any day, at a price that feels fair. Fresh, clean, and well-made — every single time. Come be one of our very first regulars.

As we open our doors, our promise stays simple:

Kofièra — bagong timpla, para sa lahat.
